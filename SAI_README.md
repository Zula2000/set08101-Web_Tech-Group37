Forest Adventure: A Text-Based Survival Game
Overview
Forest Adventure is an immersive text-based game where you find yourself lost in a mysterious forest after a hiking trip gone wrong. Your goal is to navigate through the wilderness and find your way back to civilization. Every decision you make affects your journey, with some choices helping you regain strength while others may lead to peril.

Game Features
Survival Mechanics: Monitor your health level as you navigate through the forest. Certain actions like climbing steep paths or crossing cold rivers will reduce your health, while resting or finding food will restore it.

Inventory System: Collect useful items throughout your adventure. These items are added to your inventory, which can be accessed at any time during your journey.

Save & Load: Your progress is automatically saved as you play. You can exit the game at any point and continue later by clicking "Load Game" on the main menu.

Multiple Paths: The game features different routes through the forest, each with unique challenges and discoveries. Your decisions shape your adventure.

Distinctive Locations: Explore various environments within the forest, including:

A flowing river with the sound of water
A steep, rocky hillside leading to a cave
A mysterious clearing with an abandoned cabin
A forest edge leading to the safety of a village
Game Possibilities
Path Choices
The forest presents three main paths, each leading to different adventures:

The river path may provide water and food but presents crossing challenges
The rocky hillside leads to higher ground and possibly a cave shelter
The forest clearing path may lead to an abandoned cabin with useful supplies
Resource Management
Balance your health carefully:

Resting restores health but consumes time
Difficult terrain reduces health through exertion
Finding food and water items can restore health
Exploration Rewards
Discover various items by exploring thoroughly:

Water bottle: Helps maintain hydration
Compass: Aids in navigation
Old flashlight: Useful for dark areas
Fresh fish: Provides sustenance
Endings
Your choices determine how your adventure concludes:

Reach the village safely with high health
Find civilization but arrive injured and weakened
Potentially face a game over if your health depletes entirely
How to Play
Open index.html in a web browser to start the game
Click "Start Your Journey" to begin your adventure
Read each scenario carefully and make your choice by clicking one of the buttons
Check your inventory regularly by clicking the "Inventory" button
Monitor your health level displayed at the top of the screen
Technical Details
Built with HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript
Uses localStorage for game state persistence
Designed to be responsive across different screen sizes
No external libraries or dependencies required
About This Project
Forest Adventure was developed as a web technologies assignment at Edinburgh Napier University. The game demonstrates fundamental web development concepts while providing an engaging user experience through interactive storytelling.

Enjoy your adventure in the forest, and may you find your way to safety!
