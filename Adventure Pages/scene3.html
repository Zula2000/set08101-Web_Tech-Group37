The Forest River

Health: 100 Inventory Save Game

You follow the sound of water and soon come upon a flowing river. The water is clear and looks refreshing. On the opposite bank, you can see what appears to be a dirt path, possibly leading to civilization.

The river isn't extremely wide, but the current seems strong. As you contemplate crossing, you notice some fish swimming in a calm pool near the bank.
