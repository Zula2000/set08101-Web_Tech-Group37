Game Instructions

How to Play Forest Adventure

You've awakened in a mysterious forest and must find your way to safety. Your decisions will determine your fate.

1. Making Choices

Each scene presents you with a situation and offers multiple choices. Click on a button to select your choice and continue to the next scene.

2. Health System

You begin with 100 health points. Some choices may cause you to gain or lose health. If your health reaches zero, your adventure ends.

3. Inventory

Throughout your journey, you may find useful items. These will be stored in your inventory and can help you overcome challenges.

4. Saving Progress

Your progress is automatically saved. If you need to leave, you can return later and use the "Load Game" button on the homepage to continue your adventure.
